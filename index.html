The Black Bird Field

Intermedia projects by Mozare, shaped through research and generative writing.

The field gathers six autonomous works. Each gives the browser another literary responsibility: relation can become spatial; a poem can remain hidden until approached; procedural language can retain the memory of blackout; generation can keep its trace; a remix can be made without writing code; and an interface carries an answer forward; the next encounter gives it authority again.

01 / WORKS

Six ways to enter the writing

The works share a field without sharing one interface. Each asks the browser to carry another form of attention.

01
A hypergraph research poemRelease v1.0.0

A hypergraph research poem · 2026

The Black Bird

A black bird appears beside a body, and other scenes gather around that contact. Each source, name, object, and relation receives an address without being closed into one explanation.

A digital haiga spaceRelease v1.0.1

A digital haiga space · 2026

Winter Road

Nine haiku inhabit a dark field that withholds them from a single view. A poem becomes legible through proximity, can be held briefly, then recedes as attention moves elsewhere.

An internet blackout poemEnglish edition · v2.6.0

An internet blackout poem · 2026

UNHAPPY Scenario

Software calls the route away from success an unhappy scenario. The phrase makes interruption sound manageable: a branch to be anticipated, named, and recovered from.

A bilingual generative e-poem · 2026

Grave-Machine

Material from the play Grave enters the Taroko Gorge remix lineage. Generated language remains beside a quieter runtime trace, keeping recurrence and construction in the same field.

05
A no-code platform for procedural poemsRelease v1.0.4

A no-code platform for procedural poems · 2026

TAROKO REMIXER

Taroko Gorge kept its word lists in plain view, and writers changed them to make poems of their own. TAROKO REMIXER turns those decisions into modules, so a working poem-machine can be designed, run, and exported without writing code.

Hamlet Pop-Upper
