About

Mozare is the artistic name used for the works. Mohammad Zare remains the name used for academic records, citations, rights, and professional identification.

Mohammad Zare is an Iranian poet, researcher, and product/interface practitioner. His work joins dramatic literature to browser-based forms and attends to moments when writing remains visibly attached to its sources, procedures, failures, or reader actions.

He studied Industrial Engineering at Sharif University of Technology and completed an MA in Dramatic Literature at the University of Art, Tehran. His practical and theoretical work has moved through playwriting, speculative research poetry, critical writing, translation, curatorial work, generative literature, and interface design.

The six works in this portfolio are where those trajectories currently meet. They are presented under the name Mozare; the formal name, Mohammad Zare, remains attached to citations, rights, the CV, and academic records.
