CONTEXT

The poem and the means of making it

The work continues the proposition made public by Taroko Gorge: a compact generator can be read, altered, and carried into another ground. TAROKE REMIXER expands that proposition into a compositional environment.

Material is named. Transformations are local. Line-making devices remain inspectable. Stanza and flow determine how the poem changes over time, and a completed project can leave the workbench as a standalone artifact.
